# Writing and videos

> **For:** anyone who wants the story and the reasoning behind AdaptShot, not only the reference — long-form writing and short videos about what the library can do, what it cannot, and what was actually measured.

This page is the permanent index. Videos go out on WhatsApp, X, LinkedIn and
Instagram; the detailed write-ups live on Substack. Each entry is added here
when it is published — if it is not listed here, it is not out yet.
