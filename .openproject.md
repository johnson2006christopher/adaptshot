## 🌍 Mission & Vision

### Mission
Democratize trustworthy, CPU-first, human-in-the-loop few-shot vision AI for resource-constrained environments. Enable accurate, calibrated, and offline-capable image classification with fewer than 50 labeled examples per class, requiring no GPU, no cloud connectivity, and no corporate infrastructure.

### Vision
Become the most impactful, energy-efficient, and scientifically rigorous AI library ever built. Shift the paradigm from "bigger models, more data, higher carbon" to "smarter architectures, human alignment, minimal footprint." Serve the Global South first, validate in real-world deployments, and scale globally without compromising sustainability or accessibility.

### The Big Picture
AdaptShot is not just a library. It is a proof that:
- AI can be accurate without being energy-intensive
- Uncertainty can be measured, not hidden
- Human expertise can guide models without retraining from scratch
- Open science can outperform closed, resource-hungry systems
- Carbon footprint can be tracked, minimized, and reported by default

We are building the foundation for the next era of AI: **lightweight, accountable, and human-centered**.

## 🏗️ Architecture & Design Principles

### Core Modules
```
src/adaptshot/
├── config/settings.py          # Frozen dataclass, immutable config
├── core/
│   ├── extractor.py            # Frozen backbone, CPU embedding
│   ├── similarity.py           # Cosine search (NumPy/FAISS)
│   ├── calibration.py          # Online temperature scaling, ECE
│   ├── act.py                  # Adaptive confidence thresholding
│   └── learner.py              # FewShotLearner (primary API)
├── training/
│   ├── feedback_router.py      # Human correction ingestion
│   ├── finetune.py             # CA-EWC head-only optimization
│   └── up_ugf.py               # Uncertainty-guided buffer pruning
├── utils/
│   ├── determinism.py          # Seed management, reproducibility
│   └── io.py                   # Path validation, JSON serialization
└── ui/app.py                   # Gradio pilot interface (optional)
```

### Non-Negotiable Principles
1. **CPU-First**: All operations default to `device="cpu"`. CUDA is opt-in.
2. **Memory-Bound**: Full pipeline must operate within `<250MB RAM`.
3. **Deterministic**: Fixed seeds, no non-deterministic ops, reproducible across runs.
4. **Human-in-the-Loop**: Corrections routed, weighted, and acted upon.
5. **Transparent**: Return calibrated confidence, uncertainty flags, and neighbor metadata.
6. **Carbon-Aware**: Track energy usage, minimize compute, optimize for efficiency.

## 🌱 Carbon & Energy Sustainability Directive

AdaptShot is explicitly designed to reduce AI's environmental impact. All engineering decisions must prioritize:

### Energy Efficiency
- Default to `mobilenet_v3_small` for low-power deployment
- Implement early-exit thresholds in `eco_mode`
- Avoid redundant computation; cache embeddings when safe
- Profile Joules/inference in benchmarks

### Carbon Reporting
- Estimate CO₂ equivalents using grid intensity factors
- Log energy metrics alongside accuracy/ECE
- Publish methodology transparently in `docs/`

### Design Questions
Before any feature is approved, answer:
1. Does this reduce or increase energy consumption?
2. Can it run on a 3-year-old laptop with 4GB RAM?
3. Does it require internet or cloud infrastructure?
4. How does it perform under intermittent power or thermal throttling?
5. Is the marginal accuracy gain worth the carbon cost?
